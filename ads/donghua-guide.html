Top 5 Beginner-Friendly Donghua Series To Watch If You Love Anime

If you love the fights in Demon Slayer or the progression in Solo Leveling, Chinese animation (Donghua) is your next obsession. Here are the 5 easiest series to start with today.

1. Link Click (Shiguang Dailiren) – 2D Mystery / Thriller

Two partners run a photo studio and enter clients' photographs to fulfill last wishes without altering the past. Emotional, suspenseful, and 2D animation that rivals top Japanese studios.

2. The Daily Life of the Immortal King – 2D Action / Comedy

Think Mob Psycho 100 meets Saiki K. An overpowered high schooler who defeated a demon king at age six just wants to eat crispy noodles in peace.

3. Renegade Immortal (Xian Ni) – 3D Dark Fantasy

Wang Lin starts with mediocre talent, faces betrayal, and becomes ruthless to survive and protect his family. Peak 3D CGI cultivation battles with zero mercy for enemies.

4. Soul Land (Douluo Dalu) – 3D Action / Adventure

A martial arts genius reincarnates into a world of Spirit Masters. Classic tournament arcs, squad teamwork, and a completely finished main story.

5. Scissor Seven – 2D Action / Comedy

An amnesiac hairdresser takes cheap assassination gigs using qi-controlled scissors. Short 15-minute episodes that evolve into an intense martial arts conspiracy.
